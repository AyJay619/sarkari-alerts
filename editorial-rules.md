SKIP these:
- Posts open only to ex-servicemen or retired personnel.
- Deputation-only posts (filled from other government offices, not open to the public).
- Internal promotions and departmental exams (limited/departmental competitive exams for existing employees).
- Consultant or advisor roles with fewer than 5 posts.
- Tenders.

POST these:
- Open public recruitment (anyone who meets the qualifications can apply).
- Admit cards, results, answer keys and corrections (corrigendum, date extension) for such recruitment.

If a notice fits none of the rules clearly, answer "post".
