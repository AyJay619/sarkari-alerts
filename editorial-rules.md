EDITORIAL RULES
(Plain text. Edit freely: no code needs to change. The whole file is sent to the AI with every Job / Correction notice.
The AI answers "post" or "skip". It may skip ONLY with one of the SKIP rules below, and it must name the rule by its exact NAME (the
words before the colon), word for word. If no SKIP rule clearly fits, it answers "post": it is never allowed to invent a reason.
A notice skipped by a rule is NOT alerted: it goes to the AI log and to one daily digest ("🙈 Skipped by rules") with its rule name,
so you can check the AI's judgement. Clear cases are skipped from the TITLE alone (keywords.json, "skipTitles"), without opening the PDF.
If you rename a rule here, the new name is what appears in the digest. One rule per line, written "- Name: explanation".)

SKIP these:
- Retired personnel only: the post is exclusively for retired people. It applies when ANY of these holds: only retired / superannuated persons or ex-employees can apply; only retired (or serving) officers or scientists of any organisation can apply (for example "retired Scientist-G / Scientist-F / Outstanding Scientist / Director from DRDO or other national R&D labs", retired bank or PSU officers); a superannuation / relieving / pension certificate is required. NOT this rule: a normal public recruitment that also allows or prefers retired persons.
- Ex-servicemen only: the post is exclusively for ex-servicemen / defence personnel. It applies when ANY of these holds: the reservation or vacancies are 100% for ex-servicemen, or "(ESM)" is part of the post or grade name (for example "Executives in E-I GRADE (ESM)"); the eligibility requires a military rank (JWO, Havildar, Subedar ...) or years of service in the Armed Forces (Army, Navy, Air Force) with a discharge certificate; only serving or retired defence personnel / defence officers can apply. NOT this rule: a quota inside a normal public recruitment (SSC, RRB, banks, police, PSUs ...), for example "10% vacancies reserved for ex-servicemen", age relaxation or preference: those must be posted.
- Deputation only: posts filled from other government offices, not open to the public.
- Internal promotion or departmental exam: limited/departmental competitive exams for existing employees.
- Consultant or advisor, fewer than 5 posts: consultant or advisor roles with fewer than 5 posts.
- Tender: tenders.

POST these:
- Open public recruitment (anyone who meets the qualifications can apply).
- Admit cards, results, answer keys and corrections (corrigendum, date extension) for such recruitment.

SPECIAL CASES (these are never skipped):
- Advertisement cancelled: a notice that cancels or withdraws an earlier advertisement is category Correction and "post", and the alert shows "❌ Advertisement cancelled".
- Updated vacancies: an updated or revised vacancy table, annexure or revised vacancy list of an existing recruitment is category Correction (never Job) and "post".

If a notice fits none of the SKIP rules clearly, answer "post".
