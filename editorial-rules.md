EDITORIAL RULES
(Plain text. Edit freely: no code needs to change. The whole file is sent to the AI with every Job / Correction notice.
The AI answers "post" or "skip". It may skip ONLY with one of the SKIP rules below, and it must name the rule by its exact NAME (the
words before the colon), word for word. If no SKIP rule clearly fits, it answers "post": it is never allowed to invent a reason.
If you rename a rule here, the new name is what appears in the alert. For now a "skip" notice is STILL SENT, marked
"🙈 AI says skip: <rule name>", so you can check the AI's judgement. One rule per line, written "- Name: explanation".)

SKIP these:
- Retired personnel only: posts open only to retired or superannuated employees / officers (for example "Eligibility: retired employees of ...", engagement of retirees).
- Ex-servicemen only: posts open only to ex-servicemen or ex-defence personnel.
- Deputation only: posts filled from other government offices, not open to the public.
- Internal promotion or departmental exam: limited/departmental competitive exams for existing employees.
- Consultant or advisor, fewer than 5 posts: consultant or advisor roles with fewer than 5 posts.
- Tender: tenders.

POST these:
- Open public recruitment (anyone who meets the qualifications can apply).
- Admit cards, results, answer keys and corrections (corrigendum, date extension) for such recruitment.

SPECIAL CASES (these are never skipped):
- Advertisement cancelled: a notice that cancels or withdraws an earlier advertisement is category Correction and "post", and the alert shows "❌ Advertisement cancelled".
- Updated vacancies: an updated or revised vacancy table, annexure or revised vacancy list of an existing recruitment is category Correction (never Job) and "post".
- Vacancy totals: a total is shown only when the notice clearly states the total. One row of a table (one state, one category, one post) is never used as the total.

If a notice fits none of the SKIP rules clearly, answer "post".
